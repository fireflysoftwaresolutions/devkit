---
description: The crew - who is working, what is ready to launch, and who should take a task
---

Run `dev crew` and summarize who is working (lanes, minutes), the open messages and decisions, and the mode. Then run `dev crew next` and list what is ready to launch with its command, what is queued, and what waits and why. If arguments describe a task, run `dev crew route "$ARGUMENTS"` and recommend the member with its reasons and the `dev crew brief` line. Follow the `crew` skill.
